Privacy Policy — Find the Tofu

English · 한국어

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Find the Tofu ("the App", "we", "us") is a casual hide-and-seek game where you paint a character to camouflage it into the stage and try not to get found by the seeker. This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you use the App.

The App is an offline game. We do not operate our own servers and the App does not require an account or login. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

1. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect or receive any personal information from you. The App has no backend, no sign-in, and does not transmit your game data to us.

Data created while you use the App is stored locally on your device only:

  • Game progress (unlocked stages, stars, scores, characters and skins, statistics, daily-challenge streak)
  • App preferences (language, sound volume, difficulty, low-spec mode)
  • Gallery images of your camouflaged scenes, saved into the App's private storage
  • Daily play-count data (used for the free plays / rewarded-ad refill system)

You can erase all of this at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

2. Sharing features

The App can create share images (e.g., "find my tofu" cards, replay GIFs) from your gameplay. Sharing happens only when you tap the share button, and the image goes to the app you choose in the system share sheet. Nothing is uploaded or shared automatically.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows a small number of ads using Google AdMob: a banner, an optional rewarded video (to refill daily plays or gain bonuses), and a small native ad in the exit dialog. There are no full-screen interstitial ads. AdMob is provided by Google and may collect and process information to serve and measure ads, including:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), device identifiers
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, coarse location derived from IP)
  • IP address and ad interaction data
  • Diagnostic information (such as the ad SDK's operation and performance data)

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is used for serving ads, analytics and fraud prevention. Please review Google's policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before serving personalized ads, in line with GDPR. Consent is requested when you first launch the App, and the App currently has no menu to reopen the consent choice. To change your choice, clear the App's data in your device settings or reinstall the App, and consent will be requested again on the next launch (this also erases your game progress). You can also manage ad personalization in Google's ad settings (https://adssettings.google.com) or in your device's ad settings (reset or delete the advertising ID).

Users in the United States

Depending on your state, you may have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Where a consent/opt-out form is presented, your choice is applied to ad personalization.

4. Permissions

The App requests no sensitive permissions — no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts. Network access is used only to load ads. Vibration is used for gameplay haptics (e.g., heartbeat effect).

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data retention and security

Because we have no servers, we do not store your data. All app data lives on your device and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Information processed by Google for ads is handled under Google's policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g., GDPR in the EEA/UK, CCPA/CPRA in the US), you may have rights to access, delete, or restrict processing of personal data, and to opt out of personalized ads. Because the developer holds no personal data, such requests primarily concern Google's ad data — see Google's policies above. You may also contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, within the App or store listing.
